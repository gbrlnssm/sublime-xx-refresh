### Sasha  
For Sasha, our cover girl, the need for a different kind of product is more urgent than ever.  
And she’s not alone.  

### Products Today Are Broken  
They’re irrelevant to her interests and dreams, and therefore counterproductive to her growth.  

**RUKI** helps Sasha and entrepreneurs like her to incept their first products and eventually build a company around them. These products will reimagine sleep, food, medicine, family—**all of life.**  

What they **won’t** do is unfold into an all-consuming corporation. Products don’t need to last, and neither do companies.  
All they need to do is **move us forward.**  

> Because it’s not about their growth,  
> it’s about ours.
